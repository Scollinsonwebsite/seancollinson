## 3. Logo Usage

- **Mark:** two converging lines with a dot where they meet, sky blue on a white circle (header) or white on sky (favicon).
- **Wordmark:** "Sean Collinson" in Schibsted Grotesk 800 over "Mediation Office" in Manrope 600.
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the mark on every side.
- **Minimum size:** 24px mark height on screen.
- **Don't:** recolor outside the sky palette, add effects, rotate, separate the dot from the lines, or set the wordmark in another face.

## 4. Voice & Tone

### Personality
Calm authority. Plain English. Strategic, credible, compassionate, direct. Never sensational.

### Voice chart
| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Calm | Dramatic or urgent |
| Direct | Blunt or cold |
| Credible | Boastful |
| Compassionate | Sentimental |
| Neutral | On anyone's side |

### Tone by context
| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero and calls to action | Confident, reassuring | "Your dispute does not have to control your future." |
| Process and services | Clear, practical | "Four stages, each with one purpose." |
| Confidentiality and legal limits | Careful, qualified | "California has statutes that generally protect… subject to specific exceptions." |
| Forms and errors | Specific, helpful, no apology | "Enter an email address in the format name@example.com." |
| Masterclass | Energetic but grounded | "Stay calm. Read the conflict. Move the conversation forward." |

### Writing rules
- Sentence case for buttons, labels, and most headings; Title Case only where the brief fixed a heading.
- An action keeps one name everywhere: **Schedule a Confidential Consultation**.
- Say what happens next ("The office will contact you to arrange a time"), never imply a booking that has not happened.

### Prohibited terms and claims
- "Attorney", "lawyer", "law firm", "Esq.", "legal advice" describing Sean or the practice.
- "Represent", "advocate for", or "on your side".
- "Best", "top", "#1", "leading", "guaranteed", "win".
- Unverified numbers: settlement rates, case counts, settlement totals (see `CONTENT-NEEDED.md`).
- Any implied endorsement by the FBI or the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department.

## 5. Imagery Guidelines

### Photography
- Real photographs of Sean only: no stock people, no AI-generated faces.
- Calm, professional settings: offices, conference rooms, natural or warm interior light.
- Navy and neutral wardrobe and backgrounds, which sit naturally with the sky palette.
- Leave open negative space on one side for headlines (the hero photo is the model).
- No gavels, scales of justice, staged arguments, or repeated handshakes.

### Graphics
Geometry only: the convergence lines, route lines with stops, simple diagrams. No illustrated scenes.

### Icons
Lucide, 24px grid, 1.75 stroke, inside tinted circular chips. No emoji.

## 6. Design Components

- **Buttons:** full pills, 52px tall; white on sky or photo, sky 700 elsewhere.
- **Spacing:** fluid gutter 16–40px; 16px tile gap; sections 64–112px.
- **Radius:** tiles 26px, inner cards 16px, rows and inputs 12px, controls pill.
