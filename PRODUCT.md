# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with minimal JavaScript, generated by a dependency-free Node build (`build.mjs`) from `src/`. Must run on Bluehost shared hosting with no Node.js, database, or server-side rendering. Forms post to a bundled PHP handler.

## Users

- Individuals and couples facing divorce, legal separation, custody, support, or property disputes, often stressed and unfamiliar with mediation, looking for a calm, trustworthy neutral.
- Parents working out parenting plans.
- Attorneys looking for a neutral for a client's matter.
- Executives, business owners, employers, employees, landlords, tenants, and organizations in civil, contract, partnership, workplace, or community conflict.
- Leaders, HR teams, attorneys, mediators, and public-safety professionals interested in negotiation training (Masterclass).

Their job: decide whether mediation, and Sean, are right for their dispute, then request a confidential consultation.

## Product Purpose

The website for Sean Collinson and the Mediation Office of S. Collinson. It positions Sean as an experienced neutral who helps people resolve difficult disputes without unnecessary litigation, and converts visitors into consultation requests. Secondary: promote the "Think Like a Hostage Negotiator" masterclass and a video library. Optimized for people first, conversions second, search visibility third.

## Positioning

A family and civil mediator with more than 22 years of mediation experience who is also an FBI-trained crisis negotiator (volunteer, Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department crisis-negotiation function). Crisis-negotiation discipline (listening, emotional regulation, rapport, issue identification) applied to mediation. California-focused, with virtual sessions by Zoom nationwide.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate on phones and desktops, often during a stressful life event or while managing a business dispute. Attorneys evaluate credentials quickly. The conversion path is a non-confidential consultation request form; an online scheduler may be added later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Seven primary pages: Home, About, Mediation, Consultation, Videos, Masterclass, Contact; plus legal pages and a 404.
- Sean is a neutral mediator. Never call him an attorney, lawyer, or law firm; never imply he represents either party or gives legal advice.
- No invented address, phone, email, awards, case results, licenses, pricing, testimonials, video URLs, or social links. Missing facts live as placeholders in `src/config.mjs` and are hidden in production.
- Conditional claims (96% settlement rate, 7,000–8,000+ cases, $3 billion+ in settlements) stay off unless documented, with an explanatory note.
- No implied endorsement by the FBI or Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department.
- Confidentiality statements carry a California-specific qualifier.
- No self-awarded "best" or "top" titles; no countdowns, fake scarcity, or manipulative pop-ups.
- Primary CTA "Schedule a Confidential Consultation"; secondary "Explore Mediation Services".

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mediation Office of S. Collinson; person: Sean Collinson, Family and Civil Mediator.
- Voice: calm authority, plain English, strategic, credible, compassionate, direct, never sensational.
- Visual reference supplied by the user (2026-10-01): a sky-blue, full-bleed photographic hero with a large bold sans headline, frosted-glass floating cards, and a bento grid of white rounded cards; the user chose to follow the reference's palette and to use an abstract sky (no person) for the hero.
- Existing mark: two lines that start apart and meet (convergence).

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified credentials: 22+ years of mediation; Harvard Program on Negotiation; Loyola Law School, Mediating the Litigated Case; advanced family mediation training recognized by the Florida Supreme Court; FBI-trained crisis negotiator (volunteer, LASD).
- Approved biography and positioning copy (see `src/pages/about.mjs`).
- Candidate photos in the WordPress media library (`Sean.pic44-1.png`, `sean.picdesk-2.png`), not yet approved.
- Absent and not to be fabricated: testimonials, metrics, case results, videos, contact details, pricing, CALM framework definitions.

## Product Principles

1. Calm is the product: every screen should lower the visitor's stress, not raise it.
2. Truth over persuasion: only verifiable facts; placeholders never masquerade as real information.
3. One clear next step: a confidential consultation, always reachable.
4. Neutrality is visible: copy and structure never take a side or imply representation.
5. Plain language for people in difficult moments, precise enough for attorneys.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast (including text over imagery), reduced-motion support, accessible forms with error summaries, 24px minimum touch targets.
