The RESET method

Hostage negotiators do not win arguments. They run a discipline. RESET is that discipline, adapted for a family case, from Sean's book RESET OR REPEAT.

  1. R
    R, Recognize

    Name the pattern before you answer it.

  2. E
    E, Evaluate

    Know your real interests: time, money, the kids, your peace.

  3. S
    S, Slow

    Slow the moment down. Urgency is a weapon. Do not hand it back.

  4. E
    E, Engage

    Tactical empathy that lowers the temperature without giving in.

  5. T
    T, Trade

    Trade on interests, close in writing, and make it hold.

The book teaches the mindset. This course trains the skill.

Sean Collinson is a Los Angeles family court, divorce and civil mediator and settlement neutral with more than 22 years of experience. Straight answers.

Is this legal advice?

No. This course teaches negotiation and communication strategy. It is education, not legal advice and not therapy. Sean is a mediator, not a lawyer, and a mediator is neutral and does not represent either side. For advice about your rights, consult a licensed attorney in your state. The course itself teaches you exactly when a lawyer and the court are the right tools, including a full lesson on safety and escalation.

Will this work if the other side will not cooperate?

The course is built for exactly that. Every method here assumes a high-conflict pattern across the table: baiting, scorekeeping, deadline pressure. You do not need their cooperation to change your moves, your messages, and your record.

I already have a lawyer. Is this still useful?

Yes. Lesson 7.2 is devoted to working with your lawyer like a negotiator: how to brief them, how to read a bill, and how to keep your strategy and your spending aligned. Prepared clients cost themselves less.

Does this replace mediation?

No. Mediation is where many of these cases settle, and Module 7 explains how it works and how to walk in ready. Many students use the course to prepare for a mediation with Sean or with a mediator in their own state.

How long do I keep access?

Core, Plus, and VIP all include lifetime access to the course lessons. Plus community and live sessions run while your Plus enrollment is active.

What if it is not for me?

The 14-day guarantee in Section 8 applies to every tier.

My situation involves safety concerns. Should I take this course?

If there is any concern for physical safety, threats, stalking, or coercive control, start with Lesson 8.3, which is free to watch first inside the course preview, and with a licensed attorney and local support resources. If you are ever in immediate danger, call 911. In the United States, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233.
