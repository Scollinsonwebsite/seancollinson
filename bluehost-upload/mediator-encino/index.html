Who it helps

Mediation fits Encino parents who want a detailed parenting plan built around school, activities, and two busy work schedules, and couples who need to divide a home, retirement accounts, or a business with the numbers on the table. It also fits people who simply want the process to be quieter and less expensive than a contested case.

Family court for Encino

Family law cases for Encino residents are filed with the Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles. The mediation itself does not happen at the courthouse. Once you agree, the written terms go to the court for approval.

In person or by Zoom

The Sherman Oaks office at 15301 Ventura Blvd is a short trip east along Ventura Boulevard from Encino. Prefer to stay home? Sessions are also available by Zoom.

A steady neutral for difficult conversations

Sean Collinson has more than 22 years of mediation experience helping people resolve disputes they could not settle on their own. His training includes Harvard’s Program on Negotiation and Loyola Law School’s Mediating the Litigated Case program.

He is also an FBI-trained crisis negotiator who volunteers with the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department. That work demands disciplined listening and calm under pressure, and he brings the same skills to every mediation. Meet Sean Collinson.

Sean’s volunteer crisis-negotiation service is separate from his mediation practice. Neither the FBI nor the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department endorses this practice or its services.

Questions from Encino clients

Do you have an office in Encino?

The office is in neighboring Sherman Oaks, at 15301 Ventura Blvd. Encino clients meet there in person, or by Zoom.

Can you help divide a business or retirement accounts?

Yes. Property and finances are a regular part of divorce mediation. Couples often bring in a neutral appraiser or financial professional when values are complicated. Read about property division.
